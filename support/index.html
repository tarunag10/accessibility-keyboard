Help with Accessibility Keyboard

Use this page for setup help, keyboard activation guidance, and support contact information.

Enable the Keyboard

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Go to General, then Keyboard, then Keyboards.
  • Tap Add New Keyboard and select Accessibility Keyboard.
  • When typing, press the globe key to switch keyboards.

Contact Support

Email support@codecounsel.app with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
