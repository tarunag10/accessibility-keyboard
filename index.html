Clearer typing, calmer settings.

Accessibility Keyboard provides accessible keyboard profiles, large-key layouts, high-contrast themes, haptics, and local settings for people who want an easier typing experience.

Privacy Policy Support

Privacy-first behavior

The keyboard extension is configured without full access. Typed content is handled locally by iOS keyboard extension APIs and is not sent to a server by this app.
